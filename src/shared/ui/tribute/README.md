# Tribute

> Long ago, in a Facebook office,
> some engineers were sat in front of their feed,
> when out of the open-plan there arose
> a **Walke**, and he said:
>
> _"Render me the finest UI framework in the world,
> or I shall eat your soul."_
>
> Well, the engineers looked at each other,
> and they each said... _okay._
>
> And they wrote the first framework that came to their heads,
> just so happened to be
> the best framework in the world.
> It was the best framework in the world.
>
> Look into my eyes and it's easy to see:
> useEffect data leak, memoising this shit,
> It was destiny
>
> Needless to say, the Walke was stunned.
> He open-sourced it, and walked into the night.
>
> But this is not that framework, no.
> This is just a tribute.
>
> Couldn't afford the overhead of said framework, no, no.
> This is a tribute, oh, to the best framework in the world.
> All right, it was the best, all right.
